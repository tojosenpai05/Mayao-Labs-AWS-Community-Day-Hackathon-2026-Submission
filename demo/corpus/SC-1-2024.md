This circular sets out entitlement and procedure for training and professional development.

Every officer is entitled to a minimum of seven days of structured training per calendar year. Departments are responsible for ensuring officers meet this minimum.

Applications for external training exceeding three days or RM 3,000 in cost require approval from the Head of Department and endorsement by the State Training Committee.

Officers who receive sponsored training valued above RM 10,000 are required to serve a bond period of two years from completion of the training.
