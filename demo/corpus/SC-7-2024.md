---
This circular revises the direct procurement threshold previously set in Service Circular No. 3/2022, which is hereby superseded.

The direct procurement limit is raised to RM 50,000 per transaction with effect from 1 July 2024. Acquisitions valued above RM 50,000 must proceed through quotation or open tender as prescribed by State Financial Instructions.

The prohibition on splitting a single requirement into multiple transactions remains in force.

Departments must continue to maintain a register of direct procurement transactions and submit a quarterly return to the State Treasury. The register must record the justification for each transaction above RM 20,000.
