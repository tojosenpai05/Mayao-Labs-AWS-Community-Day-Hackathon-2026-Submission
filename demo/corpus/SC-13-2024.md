This circular governs acting appointments to higher posts.

An officer may be appointed to act in a higher post when the substantive holder is on leave exceeding fourteen days or when the post is vacant.

An acting allowance is payable where the acting period exceeds twenty eight continuous days. The allowance is the difference between the officer's current salary and the minimum of the higher grade.

Acting appointments exceeding six months require the approval of the State Public Service Commission.
