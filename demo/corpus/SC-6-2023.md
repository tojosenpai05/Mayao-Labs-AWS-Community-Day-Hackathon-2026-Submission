This circular governs claims for work performed outside prescribed office hours.

Overtime is payable at 1.5 times the officer's hourly rate for work on a normal working day and 2.0 times the hourly rate for work on a rest day or public holiday.

Overtime must be authorised in writing by the Head of Department before the work is performed. Retrospective authorisation is permitted only in cases of genuine emergency and must be justified in writing.

The maximum claimable overtime is 64 hours per month. Officers in Grade 48 and above are not eligible for overtime payment.
