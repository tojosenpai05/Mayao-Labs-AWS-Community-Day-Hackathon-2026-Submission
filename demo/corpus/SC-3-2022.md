title: Service Circular No. 3/2022 - Direct Procurement Limits
dept: Sarawak State Treasury
date: 2022-01-10
superseded_by: SC-7-2024
---
This circular establishes the value threshold below which goods and services may be acquired through direct procurement without open tender.

The direct procurement limit is RM 20,000 per transaction. Any acquisition valued above this limit must proceed through quotation or open tender as prescribed by State Financial Instructions.

Splitting a single requirement into multiple transactions to remain below the threshold is prohibited and constitutes a disciplinary offence.

Departments must maintain a register of all direct procurement transactions and submit a quarterly return to the State Treasury.
