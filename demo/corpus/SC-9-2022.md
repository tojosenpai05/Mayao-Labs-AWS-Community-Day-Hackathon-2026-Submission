This circular governs medical leave for officers in the State Public Service.

An officer may be granted up to 90 days of paid medical leave in a calendar year on the certification of a registered government medical officer.

Medical leave certified by a private practitioner is limited to 15 days per calendar year unless subsequently endorsed by a government medical officer.

Medical leave exceeding 90 days in aggregate requires referral to a medical board, which shall advise on the officer's fitness to continue in service.
