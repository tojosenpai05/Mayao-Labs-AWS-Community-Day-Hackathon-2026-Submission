This circular governs study leave for officers pursuing further qualifications.

Full pay study leave may be granted for programmes directly relevant to the officer's scheme of service, for a maximum of four years for a doctoral programme and two years for a master's programme.

An officer must have completed at least three years of confirmed service to be eligible.

Officers granted full pay study leave are bonded for a period equal to twice the duration of the leave. An officer who resigns within the bond period is liable to repay the full cost of the sponsorship.
