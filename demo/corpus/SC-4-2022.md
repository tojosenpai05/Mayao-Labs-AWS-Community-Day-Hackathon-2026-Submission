This circular sets out the annual leave entitlement for officers in the Sarawak State Public Service.

Entitlement is determined by salary grade. Officers in Grade 19 to Grade 40 are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Officers in Grade 41 to Grade 43 are entitled to 25 days. Officers in Grade 44 to Grade 52 are entitled to 30 days. Officers in Grade 54 and above are entitled to 35 days.

Annual leave accrues monthly and unused leave may be carried forward to a maximum of 15 days into the following calendar year. Leave carried forward must be utilised within the first six months of the following year or it shall lapse.

Officers on probation are entitled to annual leave on a pro-rata basis from the date of appointment.
