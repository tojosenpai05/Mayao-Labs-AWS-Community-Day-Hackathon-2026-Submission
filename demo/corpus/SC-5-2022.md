This circular describes the procedure an officer must follow to apply for annual leave.

Step 1. The officer submits a leave application through the departmental leave system at least fourteen working days before the intended first day of leave. Applications submitted with less notice require written justification.

Step 2. The immediate supervisor reviews the application and verifies that the officer has sufficient leave balance and that departmental operations can continue in the officer's absence.

Step 3. The supervisor forwards the application to the Head of Department for approval. The Head of Department shall decide within five working days.

Step 4. The officer receives written confirmation of approval or rejection. An officer must not commence leave before receiving written approval.

Applications for leave exceeding fourteen consecutive days require the additional endorsement of the State Secretary's office. Emergency leave may be applied for retrospectively within three working days of returning to duty.
