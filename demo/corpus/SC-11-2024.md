This circular prescribes retention periods for official records.

Financial records including vouchers, receipts and procurement documentation must be retained for seven years from the end of the financial year to which they relate.

Personnel records must be retained for the duration of service and seventy five years thereafter.

Policy files, circulars and records of decisions are classified as permanent records and may not be disposed of. They must be transferred to the State Archives when no longer in active use.

Disposal of any record requires written authorisation from the State Archivist.
