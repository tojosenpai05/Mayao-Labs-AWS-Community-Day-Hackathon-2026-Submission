This circular sets out the disciplinary procedure for officers in the State Public Service.

Where misconduct is alleged, the Head of Department shall appoint an investigating officer to establish the facts and submit a report within thirty days.

The officer concerned must be given a written statement of the allegation and not less than twenty one days to respond in writing.

Disciplinary authority rests with the State Public Service Commission for charges that may result in dismissal or reduction in rank. Lesser penalties may be imposed by the Head of Department.
