This circular prescribes the rates and procedure for official travel claims.

The mileage rate for use of a private vehicle on official duty is RM 0.70 per kilometre. Claims must be supported by a travel log recording date, origin, destination and purpose.

Accommodation claims are subject to a nightly ceiling determined by the officer's grade. Officers in Grade 41 and below may claim up to RM 160 per night. Officers in Grade 44 and above may claim up to RM 220 per night.

All travel claims must be submitted within thirty days of completing the journey. Claims submitted after this period require the approval of the Head of Department.
