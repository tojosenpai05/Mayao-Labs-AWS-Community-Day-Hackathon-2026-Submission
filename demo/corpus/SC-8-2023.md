This circular describes the procedure for requesting information technology equipment.

Requests for standard equipment such as desktop computers, monitors and printers are submitted to the departmental IT coordinator, who consolidates requests monthly.

Requests for non-standard equipment require written justification describing the operational need and why standard equipment is insufficient.

The standard replacement cycle for desktop and portable computers is five years. Equipment replaced before the end of its cycle requires certification that the equipment is beyond economical repair.
