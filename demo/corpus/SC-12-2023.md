This circular prescribes the standard for recording meeting minutes.

Minutes must record the date, time, venue, attendance, apologies, matters discussed, decisions taken and the officer responsible for each action arising.

Draft minutes must be circulated to attendees within seven working days of the meeting. Attendees have five working days to propose corrections.

Confirmed minutes must be filed in the departmental record system and indexed by subject and date. Minutes of meetings at which policy decisions are taken are permanent records.
