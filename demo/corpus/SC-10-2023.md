This circular governs the use of official vehicles.

Official vehicles are to be used solely for official duty. Use for personal purposes is prohibited.

Every journey must be recorded in the vehicle log book, stating the date, driver, origin, destination, purpose and odometer readings. The log book is subject to audit.

Officers in Grade 54 and above may be assigned a dedicated official vehicle. All other officers requiring transport shall request a pool vehicle through the departmental transport officer.
